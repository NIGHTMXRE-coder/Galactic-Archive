Notes from the archive

  • The Clone Wars film comes before the series. It was released in cinemas as a pilot, and it's where Ahsoka is introduced — watch it first, then the seven seasons. Everything here happens well before Order 66, which is Revenge of the Sith.
  • The three Tales anthologies float. Tales of the Jedi, Tales of the Empire and Tales of the Underworld each jump around in time by design, so any placement is a compromise. These are the spots StarWars.com uses; nothing breaks if you watch them elsewhere.
  • Andor and Rebels run at the same time. They're on opposite sides of the galaxy and never touch, so the order between them is yours. Both finish just before Rogue One.
  • Book of Boba Fett is half a Mandalorian season. Episodes 5 and 6 barely feature Boba and lead straight into Mandalorian season 3. If you skip the rest, don't skip those two.
  • Short on time? The CORE entries alone are the through-line: the nine numbered films, the Clone Wars, both Andor seasons, and Rogue One. That's the story. Everything else deepens it.
  • Runtimes for series are estimates — full-season totals at average episode length, not per episode. Films are exact. Anything marked INCOMING can't be ticked off until it's out.
  • Left out on purpose: video games, novels and comics. Canon, but this is a watch order. Visions and the rest of the odds and ends live under Outer rim.
